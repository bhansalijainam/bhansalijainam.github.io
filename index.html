About

I learned software by trying to break it. Two years in QA at VOIS put me close to requirements, releases and the people waiting on them. A master's in AI at Yeshiva University added the modeling side. Now I build AI that has to hold up for a customer.

Services

Three ways to work with me.

  • Forward deployed

    I join your team or your customer's, turn a loose requirement into a scoped build, and stay through rollout. Requirement analysis, test design and defect management were my daily work at VOIS.

    • Requirements
    • Scoped builds
    • Rollout

  • Applied AI

    Fine-tuned language models, NLP, computer vision and classic machine learning, chosen for the problem. I evaluate against real data before anything ships.

    • LLM fine-tuning
    • NLP
    • Computer vision
    • Evaluation

  • Freelance

    A prototype, a model, a data analysis or an AI feature inside a web app. Python on the model side, TypeScript and JavaScript on the product side, with a clear scope agreed up front.

    • Prototypes
    • Models
    • Data analysis
    • AI features
